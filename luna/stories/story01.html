LUNA'S MAGICAL WORLD
COLLECTION 1
Luna and the Amazing Farm
STORY 01

A New Home, A New World

The beginning of Luna's magical journey

Luna was fourteen years old when she arrived at the farm with her family.

The farm stood at the edge of a quiet village, surrounded by green hills, fruit trees, and a narrow river that curved gently through the land.

Their new home was a simple wooden house with a small garden in front. Behind it stood an old wooden shed, a pond filled with little fish and frogs, and a wooden bridge that crossed the stream.

Luna loved the place immediately.

Her father was a baker in the village. Every morning, the smell of fresh bread filled their home.

Her mother worked in people's homes around the village. She often returned home tired, but she always smiled when she saw Luna.

Luna's uncle was the village postman. He traveled from house to house on his bicycle, carrying letters and small packages.

Her grandmother lived with the family too. She was quiet and thoughtful. Sometimes Luna felt that her grandmother knew secrets that nobody else knew.

Luna also had a cousin who used a wheelchair. The two girls enjoyed spending time together beside the pond, watching the ducks and talking about their dreams.

And then there was Garo.

Garo was Luna's loyal dog. Wherever Luna went, Garo followed.

On Luna's first afternoon at the farm, she walked across the wooden bridge with Garo beside her.

Beyond the bridge stood several old fruit trees. Apples, pears, plums, and cherries grew among their branches.

But one tree was different.

It stood beside the river.

It was much larger and older than every other tree on the farm. Its trunk was enormous, and its branches reached high into the sky.

Luna slowly walked toward it.

Garo suddenly stopped.

Luna looked at him.

"What's wrong, Garo?"

The dog stared at the ancient tree.

Luna placed her hand against its rough bark.

For a moment, nothing happened.

Then she heard something.

Knock.

Luna froze.

She looked at Garo.

"Did you hear that?"

Garo gave a quiet growl.

Luna pressed her ear against the tree.

Nothing.

Only the sound of the river flowing nearby.

That evening, during dinner, Luna finally asked her grandmother about the tree.

"Grandma, how old is that tree beside the river?"

Her grandmother became very still.

"Very old," she answered.

"Was it here before the farm?"

Her grandmother looked toward the window.

"Long before the farm."

Luna waited for more.

But her grandmother only smiled.

"Perhaps one day it will tell you its story itself."

Luna laughed.

"Trees can't talk."

Her grandmother looked at her carefully.

Then she whispered:

"Not yet..."

That night, Luna lay awake in her new room. Moonlight entered through the window.

She looked outside.

At first, everything was dark and quiet.

Then she saw something between the branches of the ancient tree.

A tiny light.

It appeared for only a few seconds.

Then it disappeared.

Luna sat up in bed.

Garo suddenly opened his eyes.

Both of them looked toward the window.

The ancient tree was not simply an old tree.

And Luna had just taken the first step toward discovering why.
