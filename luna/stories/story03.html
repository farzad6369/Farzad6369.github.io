LUNA'S MAGICAL WORLD

Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 03 — A Letter for the One Who Hears the Forest

Morning made the night seem impossible.

The rooster called from the yard. Her father prepared for the bakery. Her mother moved between the kitchen and the back room. Her uncle's bicycle leaned against the fence.

Yet Luna could not forget the knock in the wall.

She watched Grandmother at breakfast.

“Did you sleep?”

“Yes.”

“Did you hear anything last night?”

Grandmother's spoon stopped.

“What should I have heard?”

Luna looked down.

“Nothing.”

After breakfast, she went to the shed with Garo. She placed the pale wood on a workbench and examined the symbol in daylight.

It was not a random mark.

It looked almost like a letter.

Luna copied it into her notebook.

Then she wrote:

To the one who hears the forest.

She smiled at the strange sentence, but she continued.

“I heard a sound from the old tree. If you know what it means, please answer.”

She folded the paper.

Her uncle was leaving for the village.

“Can you deliver something?”

He read the envelope.

“To the one who hears the forest?”

“I don't know an address.”

He smiled. “Neither does the post office.”

Still, he agreed to leave it in the old mailbox near the forest road.

That afternoon Luna sat beneath the ancient tree.

Birds gathered above her.

One bird called.

Then another.

For the first time, Luna felt that the sounds carried more than simple calls. She could not explain it, but something inside her seemed to recognize them.

Garo stared toward the forest.

A second piece of pale wood lay beside his paw.

Luna picked it up.

The same symbol.

That evening her uncle returned.

“The letter is gone.”

“Who took it?”

“I don't know.”

Luna looked toward the forest.

The next morning, a folded leaf appeared on the doorstep.

Inside was one sentence:

I heard you.

Luna read it three times.

Garo sniffed the paper.

She looked at the ancient tree.

For the first time, she understood that someone—or something—had answered.
