Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 04 — Someone Behind the Fence

The footprints appeared after the rain.

They began near the forest path and ended behind the barn.

Luna crouched beside them.

They were human.

Garo sniffed the mud and growled.

A movement came from behind the fence.

Luna stood.

“Hello?”

Silence.

Then a cough.

She stepped closer.

An eye appeared between two boards.

Luna froze.

The stranger disappeared.

Garo raced through the broken section of fence.

Luna followed.

They ran between wet trees until the stranger vanished.

A torn piece of blue cloth remained on a branch.

Luna took it.

The same symbol was stitched inside.

She returned to the farm and showed Grandmother.

Grandmother's face changed.

“Where did you find this?”

“Behind the fence.”

“Did you see the person?”

“Yes.”

“Then forget him.”

“Why?”

“Because some people hide because they have something to fear.”

“That doesn't mean I should fear them.”

Grandmother folded the cloth.

“No. It means you should be careful.”

Luna watched her.

“You know the symbol.”

Grandmother looked toward the ancient tree.

“It has appeared before.”

“When?”

“A long time ago.”

“Who used it?”

Grandmother stood.

“Enough.”

That evening Luna found another footprint beside the pond.

The stranger had not left.

He was still somewhere near the farm.

And he was watching the tree.
