Story 05 — The Tree That Should Not Be Touched

The following morning Luna decided she would not touch the ancient tree.

She would only observe it.

She brought her notebook and sat on the riverbank.

Her father passed carrying a basket.

“You spend a lot of time here.”

“I like it.”

He looked at the tree.

“Your grandfather liked it too.”

Luna looked up.

“Grandfather?”

“He used to sit beneath it.”

“What did he do?”

“Listen.”

“To the river?”

Her father smiled.

“Perhaps.”

After he left, Luna examined the trunk.

Three old marks were visible beneath the moss.

One circle.

One line.

One version of the strange symbol.

A gust of wind scattered Luna's papers. One page slid beneath the tree.

She reached for it.

Her fingers touched the bark.

Knock.

She pulled back.

A thin line appeared in the trunk.

It resembled a doorway.

Luna held her breath.

Then the line vanished.

A voice came from beyond the fence.

“You should not touch that tree.”

The stranger stood among the trees.

“Who are you?”

He did not answer.

“Why are you watching us?”

“I'm watching the tree.”

“Why?”

“Because someone else is looking for what it protects.”

“What does it protect?”

The stranger stepped backward.

“You are asking the wrong question.”

He disappeared.

Luna stood alone.

For the first time, the mystery felt dangerous.

Not because of the tree.

Because someone else wanted whatever the tree was hiding.
