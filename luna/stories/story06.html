Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 06 — Garo's Little Secret

Garo had been behaving strangely.

Every afternoon he disappeared behind the shed.

Luna followed him.

He stopped beside an old wooden box.

She opened it.

Inside were a rusty buckle, a broken horseshoe, a smooth stone and a faded red cloth.

Garo pulled the cloth free.

The symbol was sewn into it.

“Where did you find this?”

Garo ran toward the river.

Under the ancient tree he began digging.

Luna knelt.

A small metal bell appeared in the soil.

It had no clapper.

She shook it.

Nothing.

“You've been hiding this?”

Garo looked away.

Luna laughed.

“You really do have secrets.”

Grandmother saw the bell that evening.

She became very quiet.

“Where did you get it?”

“Garo found it.”

Grandmother looked at the dog.

“You remembered.”

Luna frowned.

“Remembered what?”

“Nothing you need to know yet.”

“Everyone keeps saying yet.”

Grandmother smiled faintly.

“Because some answers become dangerous when they arrive too early.”

That night Luna placed the bell beside the two pieces of pale wood.

At midnight, the bell rolled across the table.

Garo stood.

From beyond the river came a faint ringing.

The bell in Luna's hand had made no sound.

Something else had answered it.
