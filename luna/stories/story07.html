LUNA'S MAGICAL WORLD

Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 07 — The Voices of Animals

The animals began behaving differently.

The horse watched the forest.

The geese refused to cross the yard after sunset.

The chickens gathered near the old tree whenever Luna approached.

One morning Luna sat beside Garo and listened.

The horse made a soft sound.

Suddenly Luna understood something—not a word, but a feeling.

Danger.

She looked at Garo.

The dog barked once.

This time she understood him.

Don't go alone.

Luna stared.

“Did you just tell me that?”

Garo tilted his head.

She laughed nervously.

“Then tell me again.”

He barked.

The goose called from the pond.

Luna felt another impression.

River.

Someone.

Watching.

She walked toward the water.

Fresh human footprints crossed the mud on the opposite bank.

The animals had known.

Luna returned to the farm and wrote everything down.

She did not claim she could speak to animals.

Not yet.

But she knew their voices were becoming clearer.

That evening Garo sat beside her.

“You knew before I did,” Luna whispered.

He rested his head on her knee.

Beyond the house, the ancient tree moved without wind.

For the first time, Luna wondered whether the tree was not simply waking up.

Perhaps it was teaching her how to listen.
