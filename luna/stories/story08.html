Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 08 — The Girl Beside the Pond

A girl was sitting beside the pond.

Luna had never seen her before.

Garo did not bark.

That made Luna even more curious.

“Hello.”

The girl looked up.

“Hello.”

“Are you from the village?”

“No.”

“Where are you from?”

“Not far.”

The answer made Luna smile.

“That isn't very helpful.”

The girl smiled back.

“My name is Mira.”

Luna sat beside her.

“Mira, do you know the forest?”

“Yes.”

“Do you know the old tree?”

Mira looked at Garo.

“I know what the animals say about it.”

Luna's heart quickened.

“What do they say?”

“That it remembers.”

“Remembers what?”

Mira handed her a folded drawing.

It showed the ancient tree, the river and a dark shape beneath the roots.

“What is that?”

“A place.”

“A place where?”

Mira stood.

“Somewhere you haven't reached yet.”

“Wait.”

Mira turned.

“Do you know the stranger?”

Her smile disappeared.

“Which stranger?”

“The man watching the farm.”

Mira looked toward the forest.

“He is not the only person watching.”

Then she walked away.

Luna unfolded the drawing again.

Beneath the roots was a shape like a doorway.

She looked at the tree.

The same shape had appeared in its bark.

Someone had drawn a map without calling it a map.

And Luna had just been given the first piece.
