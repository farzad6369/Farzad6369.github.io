Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 09 — The Boat on the Water

The old boat had been covered beside the pond for years.

That morning the cloth was gone.

Garo stood beside it.

Luna inspected the wood.

The boat was old, but the hull was solid.

Her father had warned her not to use it alone.

She found an oar inside.

A symbol had been carved into the handle.

The same symbol.

Luna stared.

“Everything here seems to know something except me.”

She pushed the boat into the water.

Garo jumped in.

“You are impossible.”

They crossed the pond slowly.

Halfway across, something moved beneath them.

The boat rocked.

Garo barked.

A wooden cylinder floated to the surface.

Luna pulled it aboard.

Inside was a rolled paper.

Only one sentence was written:

The forest remembers those who listen.

Luna looked toward the shore.

Mira stood there.

Then she vanished among the trees.

Luna returned quickly.

At the forest edge, her footprints stopped.

No trail continued.

Garo stared at the ancient tree.

Luna followed his gaze.

The tree's oldest branch pointed toward the river.

That night Luna placed the paper beside the silent bell.

For a long time nothing happened.

Then the bell moved.

Far away, a real bell answered.
