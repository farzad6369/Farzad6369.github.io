Story 10 — A Light in the Forest

Three nights later Luna saw the light again.

It appeared beyond the river.

She woke before dawn and found Garo already standing at her door.

They crossed the bridge.

Mist covered the ground.

The light moved between the trees.

Luna followed.

It stopped inside a circle of stones.

In the center lay another piece of pale wood with the strange symbol.

Luna picked it up.

A voice came from the darkness.

“You should not have followed the light.”

The stranger stepped forward.

“What is it?”

“You don't know what you are asking.”

“Then explain.”

He looked at the glowing point.

“It appears when the forest chooses someone.”

“Chooses them for what?”

He hesitated.

“To listen.”

The light floated toward Luna.

Garo barked.

The stranger shouted, “Don't touch it!”

Luna stopped.

Inside the light was something like a seed.

Then the glow vanished.

The stranger whispered, “It has chosen you.”

Luna demanded his name.

He refused.

“Tomorrow,” he said.

Then he disappeared.

Luna returned home.

Grandmother was waiting.

“You saw the light.”

“Yes.”

Grandmother closed her eyes.

“The forest has begun again.”

“What has begun?”

“A story that should have ended long ago.”

Luna looked toward the ancient tree.

Grandmother's voice became very quiet.

“And now it has found you.
