LUNA'S MAGICAL WORLD

Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 11 — What Grandmother Knew

Grandmother did not explain the next morning.

Instead, she asked Luna to help gather apples.

Luna worked silently.

At last she said, “How long have you known?”

“Known what?”

“That something is happening.”

Grandmother placed an apple in the basket.

“Longer than you have been alive.”

“Did Grandfather know?”

Grandmother stopped.

“Yes.”

The answer changed everything.

Luna lowered the apple.

“What did he know?”

Grandmother looked toward the river.

“He knew the farm was not ordinary.”

“Why?”

“Because he was asked to protect it.”

“Protect it from what?”

“From people who wanted to use what was sleeping beneath it.”

Luna remembered the symbol.

“The tree?”

Grandmother nodded.

“Not only the tree.”

She reached into an old cupboard and removed a small wooden box.

Inside was a photograph.

A much younger Grandmother stood beside Grandfather under the ancient tree.

A third person stood behind them.

The face had been scratched away.

Luna touched the photograph.

“Who is that?”

Grandmother took it back.

“Someone who made a promise.”

“What happened?”

“He broke it.”

Before Luna could ask more, a knock came at the front door.

The stranger stood outside.

Grandmother opened the door.

For the first time, Luna saw fear in her eyes.

The stranger looked at Grandmother.

“You kept it hidden.”

Grandmother answered, “And you came back.”

He glanced at Luna.

“Too late.”

Grandmother stepped between them.

“Not yet.”

The stranger left without another word.

Luna stared at Grandmother.

“What is hidden here?”

Grandmother finally said:

“Something your family has protected for generations.”

She did not say where.

But Luna knew the answer would change everything.
