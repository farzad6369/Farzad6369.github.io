LUNA'S MAGICAL WORLD

Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 12 — The Old Map

That evening Grandmother brought an old map from the attic.

It was folded inside a cloth.

The paper was brown with age.

“This belonged to your grandfather,” she said.

Luna spread it across the table.

The farm was drawn in the center.

The river.

The pond.

The forest.

But beyond the forest were places Luna had never heard of.

Seven circles surrounded the farm.

None had names.

One was crossed out.

Another carried the strange symbol.

“This isn't a village map.”

“No.”

“What is it?”

“A map of places that do not always remain in the same place.”

Luna looked at her.

“How can a place move?”

Grandmother folded one corner.

“When you are young, you think roads are permanent.”

“And when you're old?”

“You learn that some roads wait for the right person.”

Luna noticed a small mark beside the river.

A line ran from it toward the ancient tree.

“What is that?”

Grandmother covered the mark.

“Not yet.”

Luna pulled her hand back.

She remembered the word.

Yet.

That night she copied the map into her notebook.

When she finished, one detail disturbed her.

The map showed a small bridge that did not exist anymore.

It led directly beneath the ancient tree.

Luna went outside.

There was no bridge.

Only the river.

But on the opposite bank, beneath the moon, something wooden seemed to be rising from the mud.
