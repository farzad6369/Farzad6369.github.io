Story 13 — A Sound Beneath the Ground

The sound began after midnight.

Luna woke to a low vibration beneath the floor.

Garo was already awake.

The house trembled.

Not enough to shake the windows.

Enough to make the cups in the kitchen rattle.

Luna went downstairs.

Grandmother was standing in the doorway.

“You hear it.”

“Yes.”

They walked outside.

The sound came from the ground near the ancient tree.

Knock.

Then a deeper rumble.

Luna placed her palm against the soil.

It was warm.

Garo began digging.

“Let him,” Grandmother said.

Luna looked at her.

“You knew this would happen.”

“I hoped it wouldn't.”

Garo uncovered a flat stone.

A symbol was carved into it.

Grandmother knelt.

She brushed away the soil.

Under the symbol were three tiny lines.

“This is older than the house,” she whispered.

“What is it?”

“A warning.”

The ground vibrated again.

A crack appeared between the roots.

Luna stepped back.

Something below the earth was moving.

Then the sound stopped.

Silence returned.

Grandmother covered the stone.

“Tomorrow,” she said, “we begin looking for the old path.”

“Why?”

“Because the map was never meant to be read from the house.”

Luna looked at the forest.

The mystery was finally becoming a journey.
