Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 14 — A Place That Was Not on the Map

The old map led them to a place that should not have existed.

Luna, Garo and Grandmother followed the river beyond the farm.

The path narrowed.

The trees grew thicker.

After an hour, Grandmother stopped.

“Here.”

There was nothing there.

Only moss and stones.

She unfolded the map.

A mark matched a stone beside her foot.

Luna moved the moss.

A narrow wooden post appeared.

On it was the strange symbol.

She looked around.

“This isn't on any village map.”

“That is why the map was made.”

Grandmother pushed aside branches.

Behind them was a narrow passage.

It led to a clearing.

In the center stood the remains of an old stone wall.

Luna touched it.

Images seemed to flash in her mind.

People carrying lanterns.

A younger Grandmother.

A man beside the ancient tree.

Then the vision disappeared.

Luna stepped back.

“What was that?”

Grandmother was pale.

“You saw a memory.”

“A memory of what?”

“Of the last time the path opened.”

Garo suddenly barked.

Across the clearing stood the stranger.

He looked at Grandmother.

“You brought her.”

Grandmother replied, “She found the path herself.”

The stranger looked at Luna.

“Then the old rules have changed.”

Before Luna could ask what he meant, a bell rang somewhere beyond the trees.

The clearing filled with mist.

When it cleared, the stranger was gone.

Grandmother folded the map.

“We go home now.”

“But—”

“Now.”

Luna obeyed.

For the first time she understood that even Grandmother did not know what would happen next.
