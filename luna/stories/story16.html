LUNA'S MAGICAL WORLD

Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 16 — The Little Chest

The little box was heavier than it looked.

Luna waited until everyone was asleep.

Garo sat beside her.

She placed the key into the small lock.

It turned easily.

Inside was a folded cloth and a second piece of paper.

The paper carried only a drawing.

A tree.

A river.

A small chest beneath roots.

Luna looked at Garo.

“The chest.”

The dog sniffed the drawing.

The next morning she told Grandmother.

Grandmother was not surprised.

“I wondered when you would find it.”

“You knew it was there?”

“Yes.”

“Why didn't you tell me?”

“Because finding something yourself is different from being told where it is.”

Luna and Grandmother went to the ancient tree.

The drawing showed a place beneath the roots.

They searched until Grandmother found a hollow between two stones.

Luna reached inside.

Her fingers touched wood.

She pulled out a small chest.

It was old, no larger than a loaf of bread.

The same symbol was carved on the lid.

Luna looked at Grandmother.

“Open it?”

Grandmother shook her head.

“Not here.”

They carried it to the house.

That evening, Luna placed the chest on the table.

She could hear the river outside.

Garo watched.

Grandmother touched the lid.

“Your grandfather opened this once.”

“What was inside?”

“Seeds.”

“Ordinary seeds?”

Grandmother smiled.

“That is what we thought.”

Luna lifted the lid.

Inside were several small packets.

Each carried a different mark.

No one spoke.

Then one packet moved.

Just slightly.

Luna stared.

The adventure had reached the farm's soil at last.
