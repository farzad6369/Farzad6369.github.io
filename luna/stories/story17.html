LUNA'S MAGICAL WORLD

Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 17 — Something Beneath the Soil

The packets remained unopened until morning.

Grandmother examined them one by one.

Peas.

Beans.

Pumpkin.

Cucumber.

Tomato.

Potato.

Carrot.

Watermelon.

Some names were written clearly.

Others were written in a handwriting Luna did not recognize.

“Why keep seeds in a chest?”

“Because seeds carry possibilities,” Grandmother said.

“That sounds like something from a story.”

“Most important things do.”

Luna chose one packet with a simple green mark.

Grandmother stopped her.

“Wait.”

“Why?”

“Your grandfather planted one once.”

“What happened?”

“He grew something no one had seen before.”

Luna looked at the garden.

“What?”

Grandmother shook her head.

“I never saw it.”

That answer was not satisfying.

Still, Luna waited.

That afternoon she and her cousin worked in the garden.

Garo followed them.

The soil was soft after the rain.

Luna noticed a small patch beneath the old apple tree.

The earth there looked darker.

She dug gently.

Something hard struck the spade.

A stone.

Under it was a shallow hollow containing a tiny glass bead.

Inside the bead was a speck of green.

Luna held it to the sunlight.

The speck moved.

She dropped it.

The bead rolled toward the garden.

Garo chased it.

When Luna picked it up again, the green speck was gone.

Only clear glass remained.

That evening she returned to the chest.

The packet with the green mark was warm.

She did not open it.

Instead, she placed it beside the key.

Something beneath the soil outside gave a soft knock.

The mystery was no longer only beneath the tree.

It was spreading into the garden.
