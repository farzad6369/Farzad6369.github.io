LUNA'S MAGICAL WORLD

Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 18 — Seeds with a Strange Mark

The strange seeds carried symbols instead of names.

Luna spent the morning comparing them with the old map.

One mark matched the symbol on the tree.

Another resembled the bell.

A third looked like a spiral.

She asked Grandmother about it.

“I don't know all of them.”

“You don't?”

“No.”

That surprised Luna.

“Then who does?”

Grandmother smiled.

“Perhaps the seeds know.”

Luna laughed.

“Seeds don't know anything.”

Grandmother looked at her.

“Are you sure?”

That afternoon Luna opened one packet.

Inside were five pale seeds.

Each had a tiny dark mark.

She placed them on a cloth.

Garo sniffed them.

The horse became restless outside.

The chickens gathered near the window.

Even the wind seemed to change.

Luna put the seeds away.

“Something is happening.”

Grandmother nodded.

“The farm is responding.”

“Why?”

“Because you opened the chest.”

Luna looked toward the ancient tree.

The tree's leaves were moving.

Then one leaf fell onto the table.

A symbol appeared on its surface.

The same symbol as one of the seeds.

Luna picked it up.

Grandmother whispered, “Now we must be careful.”

That night Luna dreamed of a garden she had never seen.

There were trees with silver fruit.

A river flowed upward.

And somewhere in the distance, a child was calling her name.

When she woke, one of the seeds was beside her pillow.

She knew she had not put it there.

She held it tightly.

Tomorrow, she would plant it.
