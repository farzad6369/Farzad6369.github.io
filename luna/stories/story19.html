Story 19 — The First Planting

Luna chose a place near the pond.

Grandmother helped her prepare the soil.

The seed was small.

Almost too small to matter.

Luna placed it into the earth.

“Grow slowly,” she whispered.

Garo watched.

Her cousin brought water.

Her father laughed when he saw the tiny planting.

“Don't expect a tree tomorrow.”

Luna smiled.

“I won't.”

But the next morning, a green shoot had appeared.

It was taller than Luna's hand.

She stared.

Her father stared.

Her mother called Grandmother.

Everyone gathered around the garden.

The plant continued to grow.

Not quickly enough to see with the eye.

But quickly enough to make everyone uneasy.

By sunset, it had three leaves.

At midnight, Luna heard a rustling outside.

She looked through the window.

The plant had four leaves.

Garo stood beside it.

The leaves moved though the air was still.

Then the plant bent toward the ancient tree.

Luna stepped outside.

“Why are you doing that?”

The plant did not answer.

A faint green light traveled along its stem.

From the forest came a distant bell.

Grandmother appeared behind Luna.

She looked at the plant.

“It has begun.”

“What has?”

“The first planting.”

Luna remembered the old story.

Her grandfather had planted something.

Now she had done the same.

But she still did not know what would grow.

Before dawn, a tiny flower opened.

Its petals were unlike any flower Luna had seen.

And inside the flower was a miniature symbol.
