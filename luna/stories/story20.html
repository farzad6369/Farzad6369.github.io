LUNA'S MAGICAL WORLD

Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm

Story 20 — The Plant That Grew Overnight

By sunrise, the plant had become taller than Luna.

Its leaves were broad and silver-green.

The tiny flower had disappeared.

In its place hung a round green fruit.

Her father stared at it.

“I have never seen that.”

Neither had Luna.

The animals gathered around the garden.

The horse watched silently.

The chickens refused to step closer.

Garo sniffed the fruit and backed away.

Luna touched the stem.

Warm.

Grandmother arrived carrying the old map.

She looked at the fruit and immediately unfolded the paper.

One of the seven circles on the map had changed.

It now contained a green mark.

“The map is changing,” Luna whispered.

Grandmother nodded.

The fruit began to glow.

Not brightly.

Just enough to reveal lines beneath its skin.

They formed the same symbol.

Luna heard a sound from the forest.

A bell.

Then another.

Then three together.

The stranger appeared at the edge of the field.

He looked at the fruit.

“You planted it.”

Luna stepped forward.

“What is it?”

He shook his head.

“Not yet.”

Luna was tired of that answer.

“Everyone keeps saying that.”

The stranger looked at Grandmother.

“You should have stopped her.”

Grandmother replied, “It was never mine to stop.”

The fruit split.

Inside was not flesh.

There was a single seed.

It was shining.

The stranger stepped backward.

“The forest knows now.”

“What does it know?”

“That the next guardian has begun to grow.”

He disappeared before Luna could stop him.

Grandmother looked at Luna.

“Do not fear what grows.”

Luna looked at the strange plant.

“I don't.”

Then she looked toward the ancient tree.

“I just want to know why.”

Far beyond the farm, something answered with a deep knock.

The story was moving forward.
