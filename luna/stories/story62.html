LUNA'S MAGICAL WORLD

Collection 1 — Luna and the Amazing Farm
Story 62 — New Friends

Luna could not forget what had happened before. The mystery around the farm grew deeper as Luna followed the clue connected to “New Friends.” She did not rush. She watched, listened, and spoke with the people and animals around her. Garo stayed close, while her cousin noticed details that others missed. Grandmother sometimes answered a question with another question, leaving Luna certain that the older woman knew more than she said. By the end of the day, Luna had discovered another small clue. She kept it safe and promised herself she would return to it when she understood more. The secret would not reveal itself all at once. For now, Luna had another path to follow. And somewhere beyond the river, something was waiting for her.
